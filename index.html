OUR MISSION AND VISION

Pillar 01

Batch Mission

DALUYAN seeks to serve as the bridge for every ID126 CCS student to the information, opportunities, and representation they need; where concerns are carried beyond conversation and towards concrete action. We aim for a college where students know where to go, who to turn to, and where voices are met with action, not neglect.

Pillar 02

Batch Vision

We envision a batch where no student is left out of the current. A community where information moves with freedom, opportunities are within reach, and student concerns are not kept in a vacuum. Where every student has a place, a voice that carries, and a hand in shaping what comes next. CCS should not be something students simply learn to navigate alone. It should be a community that is accessible, responsive, and built for its students.
